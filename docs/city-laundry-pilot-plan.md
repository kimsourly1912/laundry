# City Laundry paper slip pilot

Use the [current paper slip plan](plan.md). Confirm the shop's blank slip and walk through one filled example with the owner. Use its Khmer and English labels, box grouping, field types and familiar layout.

1. From the shop's page, create the template. Check the saved appearance with a typical example and a crowded one.
2. Publish and assign the template. If assignment fails after publishing, retry assignment for that version.
3. On the owner's phone, sign in and tap the fields for counts, service ticks and total. Confirm that each value appears in its box. Save and show the QR.
4. Scan the QR from another phone. Tap Start in the configured bot, check the saved image, and open it again from the Telegram chat.
5. Reopen the slip from **Saved slips**, then choose **New slip** and verify a fresh blank copy.

Include a slow or interrupted save and a Telegram QR failure in testing. The owner should be able to retry without reentering the slip. Republish a template change and confirm that the earlier slip still opens with its original artwork.
